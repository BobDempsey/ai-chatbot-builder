# Refunds and cancellations

Fictional demo data. Northwind Cloud is an invented company and none of the policies below describe a real product.

## The refund window

Northwind Cloud refunds any charge in full within 30 days of the payment date. Ask from the Billing screen, or write to the support address on the invoice. Refunds are returned to the card that paid, usually within five working days.

After 30 days a charge is not refundable. The plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for.

## Cancelling a workspace

Cancelling stops the next renewal. The workspace keeps working until the end of the current period, then becomes read-only for 30 days, then is deleted.

A read-only workspace can be exported at any time. Reopening one inside the 30 days restores it exactly as it was, with no re-import.

## Refunds on annual plans

An annual plan cancelled inside the 30-day window is refunded in full. Cancelled later, it is refunded pro rata for whole unused months only, with the current month counted as used.

Tidal contracts are refunded under the contract's own terms rather than this page.

## Disputes

A chargeback raised with a bank locks the workspace while the dispute is open. Withdrawing the chargeback unlocks it. Northwind Cloud does not refund the same charge twice, so a disputed charge cannot also be refunded here.

## What is not refundable

Overage charges for storage above the plan limit are not refundable. Neither is the onboarding fee on Tidal, which is quoted separately and billed once.
