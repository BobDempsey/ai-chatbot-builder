# Billing and plans

Fictional demo data. Northwind Cloud is an invented company and none of the figures below describe a real product.

## The three plans

Northwind Cloud sells three plans. Drift costs 19 dollars per seat per month and includes 50 GB of object storage. Current costs 44 dollars per seat per month and includes 500 GB. Tidal is quoted per contract, starts at 12 seats, and includes 5 TB.

Every plan includes unlimited projects. Seats are counted on the first day of the billing period, and a seat added mid-period is charged pro rata to the day.

## Annual billing

Paying annually costs ten months for twelve. An annual contract can be switched to monthly at renewal, never in the middle of a term.

Annual invoices are issued on the anniversary of the first payment and are due within 14 days.

## Changing a plan

An upgrade takes effect immediately and the unused part of the current period is credited against the new plan. A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing period, so nothing is lost part way through a month.

A workspace cannot downgrade below the number of seats currently in use. Remove the seats first, then change the plan.

## Payment methods

Northwind Cloud accepts card payment on every plan and bank transfer on Tidal only. Cards are charged at 09:00 UTC on the renewal date. A failed card is retried after one day, then after three days, then the workspace moves to read-only.

Invoices are sent to the billing contact set in Workspace settings, which can differ from the workspace owner.

## Tax

Prices exclude tax. A valid VAT number entered in Workspace settings removes VAT from invoices issued after it is added. Earlier invoices are not reissued.
