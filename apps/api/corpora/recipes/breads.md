# Breads

Fictional demo data. Thistledown Kitchen is an invented cookbook and every recipe below is made up.

## Overnight hearth loaf

Makes one loaf. Mix 500 g of strong white flour, 350 g of water, 10 g of salt and 2 g of dried yeast. Leave covered at room temperature for 12 hours, until the surface is domed and bubbled.

Shape, rest for 45 minutes, and bake in a covered pot at 240 C for 25 minutes, then uncovered at 220 C for 15 minutes.

## Barley flatbreads

Makes 8. Rub 30 g of butter into 250 g of barley flour and 100 g of plain flour with 1 teaspoon of salt. Add 180 ml of warm water and knead for 5 minutes.

Rest the dough for 30 minutes, roll thin, and cook each flatbread in a dry pan for 90 seconds a side.

## Seeded rye

Makes one loaf. Use 300 g of rye flour to 200 g of strong white, 380 g of water, 10 g of salt and 100 g of active starter. Rye takes more water than wheat and stays sticky, which is correct.

Prove for 4 hours, then bake at 230 C for 40 minutes. Wait a full day before cutting it, or the crumb will be gummy.

## Troubleshooting

A loaf that spreads flat was over-proved: shorten the final rise by a third. A dense loaf with a tight crumb was under-proved, or the starter was not lively. A pale crust means the oven was not hot enough at the moment the loaf went in.
