# Weeknight suppers

Fictional demo data. Thistledown Kitchen is an invented cookbook and every recipe below is made up.

## Ember pot noodles

Serves 2. Cook 200 g of wide rice noodles for 4 minutes, then drain. Fry 2 chopped shallots and 3 cloves of garlic in 2 tablespoons of groundnut oil until they take colour, about 4 minutes.

Add 1 tablespoon of smoked chilli paste, 2 tablespoons of dark soy and 150 ml of stock. Simmer for 3 minutes, fold the noodles through, and finish with a handful of coriander.

## Thistledown fish pie

Serves 4. Poach 500 g of white fish in 400 ml of milk with a bay leaf for 6 minutes, then lift the fish out and keep the milk.

Make a roux with 40 g of butter and 40 g of flour, whisk the poaching milk in, and cook for 5 minutes until it coats a spoon. Fold in the fish and 100 g of peas.

Top with mashed potato and bake at 200 C for 25 minutes, until the top browns.

## Nine-minute green soup

Serves 2. Soften 1 chopped leek in butter for 3 minutes. Add 300 g of frozen peas and 500 ml of stock, bring to the boil and cook for 4 minutes.

Blend with 50 g of spinach and the juice of half a lemon. Season and serve. It does not reheat well, so make what will be eaten.

## Storecupboard beans

Serves 3. Fry 1 sliced onion for 6 minutes, add 2 teaspoons of smoked paprika and a tin of chopped tomatoes, then two tins of drained white beans. Simmer for 12 minutes and finish with a spoonful of sherry vinegar.
