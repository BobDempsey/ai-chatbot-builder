# Time off

Fictional demo data. Marrowbone Labs is an invented company and none of the policies below describe a real employer.

## Annual leave

Every employee at Marrowbone Labs gets 28 days of paid leave a year, plus public holidays in the country they are employed in. Leave accrues monthly and is available from the first day.

Up to 5 unused days carry into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Anything above 5 is lost at the year end.

## Booking leave

Book leave in Sprig, the people system, at least 5 working days ahead for anything up to a week, and 20 working days ahead for anything longer. A manager answers within 3 working days.

Leave already approved is not cancelled by the company except in a declared incident, and then the days go back into the balance.

## Sick leave

Report sickness to a manager before 10:00 on the first day. The first 7 days need no note. From day 8 a doctor's note is required, sent to people@marrowbone.example.

Marrowbone Labs pays full salary for the first 20 working days of sickness in any rolling 12 months, then statutory pay after that.

## Parental leave

A new parent gets 16 weeks at full pay, taken any time in the first 18 months, and it can be split into at most three blocks. It applies equally whoever gave birth, and to adoption.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 3 months can be requested after 2 years of service. It is approved by the department lead and does not break continuous service.
