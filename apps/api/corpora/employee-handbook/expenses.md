# Expenses

Fictional demo data. Marrowbone Labs is an invented company and none of the policies below describe a real employer.

## Claiming

Claim in Sprig within 60 days of the spend. A claim needs a receipt above 25 dollars and a one-line reason in every case. Claims approved by Friday are paid the following Thursday.

A claim older than 60 days needs a department lead's written approval before finance will look at it.

## Travel

Book flights under 6 hours in economy. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class needs the department lead's approval before booking, not after.

Hotels are capped at 220 dollars a night in London, New York and Zurich, and 150 dollars a night everywhere else. Anything above the cap needs a reason on the claim.

## Meals

While travelling, Marrowbone Labs reimburses up to 60 dollars a day for meals without itemising them. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

A team meal for four or more people is claimed by the most senior person present and needs the attendee list on the claim.

## Equipment

Every employee has a 1,500 dollar equipment budget on joining and a further 500 dollars each year after. It covers a desk, a chair, a monitor and peripherals. It does not cover a phone.

Equipment bought with the budget belongs to Marrowbone Labs for the first 2 years and to the employee after that.

## Home internet

Marrowbone Labs pays 40 dollars a month towards home internet for anyone working remotely more than 2 days a week. It is claimed once a quarter rather than monthly.
