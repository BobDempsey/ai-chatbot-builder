# Remote work

Fictional demo data. Marrowbone Labs is an invented company and none of the policies below describe a real employer.

## Where people work

Marrowbone Labs is remote first. Nobody is required in an office on a fixed day. Teams that want to meet in person book a week together and the company pays the travel.

The London and Lisbon offices are open to anyone who prefers a desk, with no booking needed below 20 people.

## Core hours

Core hours are 11:00 to 15:00 UK time. Meetings are scheduled inside them, and anything outside them is optional to attend and recorded.

Someone whose working day cannot overlap the core hours agrees a written alternative with their manager, reviewed every 6 months.

## Working from another country

Working from another country is allowed for up to 60 days a year without paperwork, so long as it is not a country Marrowbone Labs has no entity in and a sanctions list does not name.

Beyond 60 days, people@marrowbone.example must approve it in advance, because tax residency changes at that point.

## Equipment and security

A company laptop is required for company work. Personal machines are not permitted, even for a single day.

A lost or stolen laptop is reported to security@marrowbone.example within 4 hours, at any hour, and the device is wiped remotely.

## Meeting rules

Every meeting has an agenda in the invitation or it can be declined without explanation. A meeting of more than 8 people needs a written summary within one working day.
